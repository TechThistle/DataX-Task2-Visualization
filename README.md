## Interview Questions & Answers

### 1. What is the importance of data visualization?
Data visualization transforms complex numeric datasets into clear visual formats like charts and dashboards. It enables quick pattern recognition, trend analysis, and faster data-driven decision-making for stakeholders.

### 2. When do you use a pie chart vs. a bar chart?
- **Pie / Donut Chart:** Best used for displaying proportional split or composition of a whole (100%) with 2 to 5 distinct categories (e.g., Regional sales split).
- **Bar Chart:** Best used for comparing discrete categorical variables, especially when dealing with numerous categories or longer category labels (e.g., Sales by Product Category/Sub-category).

### 3. How do you make visualizations more engaging?
- Use consistent, purposeful color schemes without visual clutter.
- Add dynamic filters and slicers for user interaction.
- Provide clear context using bold titles, KPI summary cards, and informative tooltips.

### 4. What is data storytelling?
Data storytelling combines data, visuals, and narrative context to explain what happened in the data, why it happened, and what actionable business steps should be taken next.

### 5. How do you avoid misleading visualizations?
- Ensure the Y-axis starts at zero for bar/column charts to avoid scale distortion.
- Maintain accurate proportional scaling.
- Avoid unnecessary 3D elements and decorative effects that distort perception.

### 6. What are best practices in dashboard design?
- Follow an **F-Shape / Z-Shape layout** (place key KPIs at the top-left).
- Maintain visual hierarchy and consistent color coding.
- Eliminate redundant gridlines, labels, and chart decorations.

### 7. What tools have you used for visualization?
- **Power BI / Tableau** (Dashboard design & interactive storytelling)
- **Python (Matplotlib & Seaborn)** (Exploratory Data Analysis)
